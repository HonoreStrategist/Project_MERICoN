Corporate Overview

Engineered for Precision, Driven by Operational Reliability

MERICoN Integrated Engineering Ltd is an engineering and technical services company whose main business activity is Activities of Consultants Other Than Architecture Management Consultants. The company provides Engineering and multidisciplinary engineering such as: technical, industrial, electrical, electronic, mechanical, construction, equipment, cleaning, design, research and development, and technical consulting services.

MERICoN's activities include engineering design and consulting for machinery, industrial processes and industrial plant projects, industrial design, research and development in engineering and technology, cleaning of industrial machinery, leasing of construction and civil-engineering machinery, electrical-material telecommunications infrastructure, supply, electronics repair and maintenance, and other technical activities.

Our Mission

MERICoN integrated engineering ltd, our mission is to provide reliable, innovative, safe and cost effective engineering and technical solutions that improve the performance, reliability and sustainability of industrial, commercial, residential and infrastructure projects. MERICoN aims to combine engineering knowledge, practical technical skills and modern technology to solve client’s problems and deliver solutions that meet required technical and operational standards.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and leading integrated engineering and technical solutions company in Rwanda and the wider region, recognized for engineering excellence, innovation, reliability, safety and customer satisfaction.
